A little about us.

We wanted somewhere we’d happily spend an afternoon. So we put the kettle on and started there.

Coffeehouse began with a fairly modest wish list: good coffee, comfortable seats, and a welcome that doesn’t depend on knowing the difference between a flat white and a cortado. Somewhere you can become a regular, even if your order changes every time.

We enjoy the details: finding a coffee we’re excited to share, getting the first batch of baking out of the oven, and remembering who takes an extra shot. Most of that work happens before you arrive. Your part is simply to find a seat and enjoy it.

Our menu leaves room for both familiar favourites and the occasional experiment. Some days call for trying a different roast. Other days call for exactly the same cappuccino you had yesterday. We understand both.

Mostly, we like what happens around a cup of coffee. Old friends catch up. A rainy walk gets interrupted. Someone opens a book and forgets to check their phone. That’s the sort of place we’re trying to make.
